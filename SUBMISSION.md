## NASA Resources Cited & Integrated
1. **NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)**  
   *URL:* [https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/)  
   *Use:* Grounded 8 health domains and deep-space biological research parameters.
2. **NASA OSDR Visualization Portal**  
   *URL:* [https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/](https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/)  
   *Use:* Directed multi-metric telemetry visualization and normal baseline safety bands.
3. **NASA Environmental Data App (EDA)**  
   *URL:* [https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/eda/](https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/)  
   *Use:* Grounded Environmental Conditions (Cabin $CO_2$) and Radiation Exposure flux monitoring.
4. **NASA OSDR OSD-919**  
   *URL:* [https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-919](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-919)  
   *Use:* Grounded Bone and Physical Health domain (collagen $NTx$ resorption biomarker).
5. **NASA OSDR OSD-918**  
   *URL:* [https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-918](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-918)  
   *Use:* Grounded Immune and Blood Indicator domain ($TNF\text{-}\alpha$ cytokine profiles).
