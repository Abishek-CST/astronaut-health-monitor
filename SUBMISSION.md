## Project summary draft

**Working title:** Astronaut Health Monitor — Offline-First Mission Health Dashboard

**Short description:** An educational prototype that lets users review synthetic health observations, explore trends, and inspect transparent review flags. Its offline-first design queues selected records during network interruptions and synchronizes them when connectivity returns.

**Problem addressed:** Connectivity interruptions can make it harder to access or update digital records in remote environments. This prototype explores how a lightweight interface, local queue, and clear synchronization status can support continuity.

**Innovation to demonstrate:** Offline-first interaction, explainable review flags, data provenance labels, and a design that can be adapted for remote Earth-based settings.

**Limitations:** The prototype uses simulated data unless otherwise documented. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose conditions, and is not validated for real astronaut operations.
